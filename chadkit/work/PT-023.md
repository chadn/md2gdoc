## Decisions

- Not pinned, for a later task if anyone needs them: a list item holding several paragraphs or a code block
  (5.2), disallowed HTML tags other than `<script>` (6.11), a tab as indentation (2.2).

- No normalizer rule added. Everything new is either content that survives in a different spelling (setext,
  `_`, entities, autolinks) or content Google drops (titles, `<script>`, an image's URL), and neither is noise the
  normalizer could reverse without rewriting what a person wrote. The empty `>` lines around a quote could be
  dropped by a rule; left as a documented loss until someone syncs quotes.
- Status `tested` for every pinned row, with the loss in the row: the vocabulary's `tested` is "a fixture pair
  proves it", and `workaround` is reserved for the project's own treatments (push form, normalizer).
- Fixture 10 carries `.src.md` because the push form adds a hard break to indented code's first line, as to any
  paragraph line followed by another; the other three are pairs the push form leaves alone.
- The three bullet markers on consecutive lines, then two numbered lists after blank lines, came back as one run
  of lines with no blanks (fixture 10); fixture 12 was added to show that each marker alone, and nested mixed
  markers, survive.
