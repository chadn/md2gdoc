export's two trailing spaces except the last line of the last list, as in fixture 04.

## Item 2: a matching `version` is not proof on its own

What `files.get(fields="version,modifiedTime")` returned, each run in its own order, cited to the output files:

- `push` run (push.out.txt): `files.create` gave version 5; after one export still 5; after a second export 6
  (whether the export or the create's own late step moved it, the run cannot tell); after a style-only
  `batchUpdate` 6; after an `insertText` `batchUpdate` 6, `modifiedTime` unchanged throughout; after
  `files.update` 7 at once, with a new `modifiedTime`; a minute later still 7 (line 11).
- `steps` run on the same Doc, each read 15 seconds after its step (steps.out.txt lines 1-7): 7 before; 7 after
  nothing; 7 after an export; 7 after an italic `batchUpdate`; 7 after an `insertText` `batchUpdate`, with
  `modifiedTime` unchanged since the push run's update, about 30 seconds after the italic edit; 8 after
  `files.update` with a new `modifiedTime`; 10 thirty seconds later with a `modifiedTime` 0.4 seconds after the
  update's, so the two extra steps belong to the update itself, as PT-003 saw (4 to 7, 5 to 7) with no Docs API
  edit in play.
- `inspect` a minute later (lines 8-14): still 10; `revisions.list` shows a revision written at the time of the
  `batchUpdate` calls (id 6, 20:56:05) and of each `files.update` (ids 4 and 7), so the Docs API edits were real
  and recorded, yet neither `version` nor `modifiedTime` moved for them within about 30 seconds.
  `headRevisionId` is absent from every read of a native Doc.

What this settles: a matching `version` does not prove the Doc unchanged, so the push guard cannot skip the
export comparison on its word; written into [docs/design.md](../../docs/design.md) item 2 and the push-guard
bullet as a caveat, the guard's shape left to PT-007. gdoc's reliance on `version` alone
([docs/similar.md](../../docs/similar.md)) is unsafe by this evidence. What it does not settle: whether and how
soon an edit made in the Docs UI, the `hand-edited` case the guard exists for, moves `version`; that reading is
PT-018 on the kept Doc, with the command below at once and again a minute later.

